What is Scribe?

For many LGBT+ people, the internet has offered a space to find community, language, and recognition that everyday life doesn't always provide. But as algorithms reward outrage and amplify extreme opinions, even those spaces can feel hostile. Scribe is an alternative platform for discussing being queer. Intended as a quieter, intimate space, each prompt draws from queer thinkers, writers, essayists, and artists, presenting their principles and insights as starting points for reflection.
